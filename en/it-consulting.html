IT consulting for processes, architecture and controlled delivery

We review business processes, IT landscape, development workflow, architecture and implementation to find constraints, reduce operational and transaction costs, and make IT decisions more transparent.

Business processes and IT landscape

We analyze processes, systems, manual work and points of loss to understand where costs can be reduced, operations accelerated and observability improved.

Development workflow

We review requirements, planning, delivery, quality and team collaboration when the speed or quality of software development does not satisfy the business.

Architecture and implementation

We assess architecture and codebase before product scaling, complex integration or acquisition of an IT startup.

Consulting is useful when the problem already affects speed, cost or controllability

The work is built around the actual context: where time is lost, why delivery is slower than expected, which architectural constraints may block growth and what should change before the next major step.

When a decision is needed before scaling, integration or buying a product

Architecture and implementation audit helps reveal technical debt, scaling limits, integration risks and hidden issues that are difficult to evaluate from a presentation or demo.

When a large customer needs an IT factory, not fragmented development

We help design a production model for software products or a digital platform: roles, processes, quality control, architecture principles and management rhythm.

If IT has become hard to explain, control or scale, it should be reviewed before the next decision

The first discussion can define the entry point: business processes and IT landscape, development workflow, architecture audit or IT factory design.
