Axionica Capital

We manage a startup portfolio and help investors increase return on investment through expertise, transparent processes, analytics and active work with projects.

What Capital Helps Solve

Capital helps look at a startup as an asset: through the market, product, team, economics, risks, checkpoints and the next realistic step.

01

Assessing Project Potential

We help understand how ready a startup is for growth: whether there is a clear market, a validated problem, a working product, a strong team, economics and grounds for scaling.

02

Managing Stage Transitions

We define what needs to change for the project to move further: from idea to MVP, from MVP to first sales, from first sales to scaling, from growth to an investment or strategic transaction.

03

Portfolio Analytics

We help investors see the state of projects not through impressions and fragmented reports, but through clear criteria, data, risks, checkpoints and regular dynamics.

How We Assess a Startup as an Investment Asset

For an investor, the idea alone is not enough. The team's ability to execute the plan, validated demand, economics, decision quality and a clear return scenario matter.

01

Market

Whether there is a sufficiently large and clear market where the project can grow.

02

Product

Whether the product solves a real problem and whether there are signs of demand.

03

Team

Whether the team can quickly test hypotheses, make decisions and bring tasks to completion.

04

Economics

Whether the revenue, cost, margin and potential return on investment model is clear.

05

Risks

Which product, market, legal, technology and operational risks may limit growth.

06

Exit Scenario

Whether it is clear how an investor may receive a return: value growth, dividends, sale of a stake, strategic transaction or another scenario.

Portfolio Work Tools

We use Axionica's internal framework for project assessment, decision-making and startup support.

01

Axiom Matrix

Assesses project maturity across key dimensions: market, product, team, economics, risks, data and readiness for scaling.

Result: a clear map of the project's strengths and weaknesses.

02

Axiom Gate

Fixes checkpoints for transitions between stages: idea, MVP, first sales, scaling, preparation for a transaction.

Result: it is clear what must be completed to move further.

03

Axiom Board

Helps support a project after assessment or investment: regular decisions, expertise, risk control and help for the team.

Result: the investor sees not only reporting, but managed work with the project.

For the investor, this reduces dependence on intuition and fragmented reports. For the startup, it gives clear rules for moving to the next stage.

What an Investor or Founder Gets as an Output

Capital helps turn a startup discussion from a set of opinions into a structured assessment of the project, risks and next step.

For the Investor

  • a project maturity map;
  • a list of key risks;
  • an assessment of strengths and weaknesses;
  • criteria for moving to the next stage;
  • clear logic for supporting the project;
  • a basis for making an investment or management decision.

For the Founder

  • an understanding of how the project looks from the investor's side;
  • a list of gaps in product, market, economics or packaging;
  • recommendations for preparing for negotiations;
  • a structure for investor materials;
  • a plan for improving investment readiness;
  • a clearer growth or transaction scenario.
