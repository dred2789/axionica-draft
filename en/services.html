Services you can start with at Axionica

This page groups the services that can already be discussed in concrete terms: discovery, custom development, IT consulting, startup tracking and investor packaging.

These are the services that can be taken into work right now

This section is about practical work: reducing start-up risk, building a clear delivery setup, bringing order to existing IT processes and preparing a startup for the next step.

Discovery / audit Custom development IT consulting Startup tracking Investor packaging

Discovery / audit

Helps avoid unnecessary budget burn and clarifies scope, architecture, risks and roadmap.

This is usually the right starting point when the project is not yet structured, the scope is uncertain and the cost of an early mistake is too high.

Open service page

Custom development

Best suited for products that are already clear enough to move into implementation with a defined structure, not just code output.

This makes sense when priorities are already defined and the team needs a clear delivery setup with explicit roles, stages and pace.

Open service page

IT consulting

Reviews of processes, architecture and project management aimed at better delivery transparency.

It becomes relevant when development is already running, but the business lacks clarity around process, architecture or management quality.

Open service page

Startup tracking

Helps a team work in a clearer rhythm, see bottlenecks and keep focus across hypotheses, product work and growth.

This fits when a startup is already moving, but needs outside cadence, prioritization support and a more disciplined path toward the next stage.

Open service page

Investor packaging

Helps bring narrative, economics, risks and supporting materials into order before conversations with investors or strategic partners.

It becomes useful when the product can no longer be shown “as is” and the team needs a more structured and convincing way to explain the market, model and growth potential.

Open service page

The services on this page solve different kinds of tasks, but all of them can be valid entry points into work

Some of them are tied to product launch and delivery management, while others are tied to startup momentum, the next stage and preparation for investor conversations.

The formats differ, but they are unified by the same goal: helping a team move forward with more clarity, more calm and fewer unnecessary decisions.
