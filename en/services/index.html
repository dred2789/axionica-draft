Services you can start with at Axionica

This page groups the services that can already be discussed in concrete terms: discovery, custom development, IT consulting, AI process automation, AI training, startup tracking and investor packaging.

Discovery / audit

Helps avoid unnecessary budget burn and clarifies scope, architecture, risks and roadmap.

This is usually the right starting point when the project is not yet structured, the scope is uncertain and the cost of an early mistake is too high.

Open service page

Custom development

Best suited for products that are already clear enough to move into implementation with a defined structure, not just code output.

This makes sense when priorities are already defined and the team needs a clear delivery setup with explicit roles, stages and pace.

Open service page

IT consulting

Reviews of processes, architecture and project management aimed at better delivery transparency.

It becomes relevant when development is already running, but the business lacks clarity around process, architecture or management quality.

Open service page

AI process automation

Helps reduce manual load in repeatable processes and implement AI tools with clear result metrics.

We start with a process audit and “before” metrics, automate the selected area and measure the “after” result.

Open service page

AI employee training

Helps the team learn to apply AI tools to real work tasks without constant external dependency.

Training is built around employee scenarios, practical tasks and rules for checking the result.

Open service page

Startup tracking

Helps a team work in a clearer rhythm, see bottlenecks and keep focus across hypotheses, product work and growth.

This fits when a startup is already moving, but needs outside cadence, prioritization support and a more disciplined path toward the next stage.

Open service page

Investor packaging

Helps bring narrative, economics, risks and supporting materials into order before conversations with investors or strategic partners.

It becomes useful when the product can no longer be shown “as is” and the team needs a more structured and convincing way to explain the market, model and growth potential.

Open service page

How to choose an entry point

If a specific service is still hard to choose, it is often better to start with the right direction and then define the entry point inside it.

Product and delivery -> Axionica Studio IT consulting, AI automation and employee training -> Axionica Consulting Growth and the next stage -> Axionica Accelerator Project maturity and investment logic -> Axionica Capital

If it is not obvious where to start, the best first step is a short Telegram conversation

In many cases, one early discussion is enough to understand whether the project needs discovery, custom development, AI automation, AI training, consulting around an existing delivery setup, tracking or investor preparation.
