Axionica Accelerator

We accelerate startup development: test hypotheses, strengthen the product, build the growth track, package the project and prepare it for investment.

Three Accelerator tasks

01

Tracking

We regularly help the team keep focus: which hypotheses to validate, which metrics to review, which decisions to make and what to do next.

Result: the startup moves not by intuition, but by a clear plan of checks and decisions.

02

Packaging

We help explain the product to the market, customers, partners and investors: through the business model, financial model, presentation, pitch, market description, risks and growth logic.

Result: the project becomes clear not only to the team, but also to external participants.

03

Growth

We help move from first market signals to repeatable sales, product scaling and preparation for the next stage.

Result: the team understands what exactly must change in product, sales, processes and economics for further growth.

At which stages we get involved

Idea

There is an idea, but it is not yet clear who needs it, how large the problem is and where to start.

We help formulate the target audience, product value, key hypotheses and the first validation plan.

MVP

The product can already be shown, but it is unclear which scenarios matter, what to validate and which metrics to count.

We help collect feedback, identify key scenarios, define the next development scope and remove what is unnecessary.

First sales

There are first customers, pilots or market interest, but growth has not yet become repeatable.

We help work through positioning, funnel, sales channels, economics, customer value and development priorities.

Scaling and investment

The project needs the next stage: growth, partnerships, investment or sale of a stake.

We help prepare materials, the financial model, argumentation, roadmap and a clear project story for negotiations.

What happens in project work

Accelerator is not motivational calls and not a promise of quick growth. It is regular work with hypotheses, metrics, product, market and team decisions.

In the work, we analyze:

  • value proposition and target audience;
  • product hypotheses and validation plan;
  • business model and project economics;
  • sales, channels and first deals;
  • growth metrics and constraints;
  • materials for partners and investors;
  • risks of the next stage;
  • decisions the team needs to make now, not someday later.

What the startup gets as an outcome

The result of Accelerator work is not just a list of advice. The team receives structure, materials and management focus for the next stage.

Focus for the next stage

The team understands which hypotheses to validate, which metrics to count, which materials to prepare and which decisions to make in order to move further not chaotically, but along a manageable track.

Hypothesis map

A list of key product, market and commercial hypotheses with validation priorities.

Tracking plan

A clear working rhythm: which decisions are made, which metrics are reviewed and what is validated next.

Financial model

The logic of revenue, costs, margin, unit economics and growth scenarios.

Pitch deck

An investor or partner presentation that explains the market, product, team, model, risks and potential.

Growth roadmap

A plan for developing the product, sales, team and key metrics for the next stage.

Negotiation preparation

Argumentation for customers, partners or investors: what has already been confirmed, where the risks are and why the project can grow.
