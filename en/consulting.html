Axionica Consulting

We analyze and optimize business processes, reduce costs and identify revenue growth points. We help companies implement AI tools and train teams to use them in real work.

Axionica Consulting services

The direction has three practical entry points: reviewing the current IT context, applying AI to repeatable business workflows and training employees to work with AI tools.

IT consulting

We help make IT processes more transparent, efficient and manageable.

We analyze processes, the team, architecture and tools. We find bottlenecks and growth points, then form practical recommendations to reduce costs, increase development speed and improve IT manageability.

Learn more about IT consulting

AI process automation

We reduce manual load in repeatable business processes with AI tools.

We analyze the process, capture “before” metrics, design AI automation, implement the solution and evaluate the “after” effect. This fits tasks where measurable business results matter.

Learn more about AI automation

Employee training for AI work

We train teams to apply AI tools in real work tasks.

We show how to use AI in daily processes: for analysis, texts, documents, finding solutions and automating routine work. We help form a shared approach, practical skills and a base for further AI implementation inside departments.

What we examine in Consulting

Consulting is needed when the team is working and tasks are being completed, but the business does not understand why development is slow, where losses appear, which processes can be automated and which changes will have a real effect.

Diagnostic areas

  • development processes and task management;
  • product architecture, integrations and technical constraints;
  • roles, areas of responsibility and communication inside the team;
  • transparency of timelines, budget, priorities and backlog;
  • repeatable manual operations in business processes;
  • potential for AI automation;
  • quality of documentation and knowledge transfer;
  • metrics that can be used to assess the effect of changes.
What the client receives

As a result, the client receives not a presentation “about digital transformation”, but an applied picture of the current state: where the bottlenecks are, what can be improved quickly, what requires systemic changes, which processes should be automated, where AI can have an effect and how to measure the result.

When Axionica Consulting is needed

01

The IT team is working, but manageability is low

There are many tasks, timelines change, priorities are disputed, and it is hard for the business to understand project status and the reasons for delays. We examine task management, roles, communication, backlog and result control.

02

Development has become expensive and unpredictable

The budget is growing, timelines are shifting, and the result is hard to connect with business effect. We help separate truly important tasks from excess scope and build a more transparent development management model.

03

Processes contain a lot of manual work

Employees move data between systems, prepare reports, reconcile documents and process repeatable requests. We find areas where automation or AI can reduce load and create a measurable effect.

04

The company wants to implement AI, but it is unclear where

We do not start by choosing a fashionable tool. First we examine workflows, data, constraints and metrics, and then define where AI can actually speed up work, reduce errors or improve decision quality.

05

Architecture slows development

The system has accumulated custom changes, integrations, manual workarounds and dependencies on individual employees. We review the architecture and propose a clear improvement plan.

06

The team needs to learn how to work with AI

We help employees apply AI not in theory, but in real work tasks: analysis, documents, communication, finding solutions, preparing materials and automating routine work.

Work formats

We choose the format for the task: from a short external view to supporting changes inside the team.

01

Express audit

Fits when a company needs a quick external view of one area: process, team, architecture or an AI case.

Result: a list of problems, quick improvements and recommendations for the next step.

02

Pre-project diagnostics

Fits when a company needs to understand the current situation more deeply before automation, development, AI implementation or process restructuring.

Result: current-state map, target model, change roadmap, effect estimate and implementation priorities.

03

Change support

Fits when the company needs not only diagnostics, but help with implementation: restructuring processes, launching automation, training the team and controlling the result.

Result: changes do not remain in a report, but move into the team’s working practice.

What the client receives at the end

Consulting should end not with general recommendations, but with clear solutions that can be implemented, discussed with the team and tied to business effect.

Practical change plan

The client receives the structure of the current situation, a list of bottlenecks, improvement priorities and a clear next step: what to change, in what sequence and by which metrics to evaluate the result.

Current-state map

Description of processes, roles, systems, problem areas and constraints.

List of bottlenecks

Specific places where speed, money, quality or manageability are being lost.

Change roadmap

Improvement plan with priorities: what to do quickly, what requires preparation and what can be deferred.

AI case map

List of processes where AI or automation can have an effect, with an estimate of complexity and value.

Effect metrics

Indicators that can be used to compare the state before and after changes.

Implementation recommendations

Practical plan: which solutions to implement, with which roles, in what sequence and with which risks.
